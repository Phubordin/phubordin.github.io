    2. Prepared and presented B2C sales performance reports (daily and monthly) to support management’s strategic decision-making.

    3. Created KPI summary reports for sales representatives by region, monitored daily and quarterly performance trends.

    4. Coordinated with Internal and External Stakeholders
      Details
      • Coordinated and scheduled quarterly cross-departmental meetings.
      • Prepared data and scheduled weekly sales team meetings.
      • Trained sales representatives and product consultants on data entry procedures and the use of the Monitor Sales program
      • Coordinated with external vendors to resolve issues related to subscribed business software and applications.

    5. Additional Assigned Responsibilities
      Details
      • Created provincial sales reports as interactive dashboards for performance monitoring.
      • Gathered and compared sales targets across multiple channels to analyze performance over different time periods.
      • Prepared and delivered internal sales data support to company teams in a timely and accurate manner.
      • Updated Customer Database After Changes for Marketing and Sales Team Usage
